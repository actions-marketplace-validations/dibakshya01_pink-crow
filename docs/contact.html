Responsible use

pink-crow is a defensive tool. Its detectors and the attack fixtures in this repo exist to help defenders recognize and limit abuse of AI systems — not to build better attacks. Please use it to protect the chatbots and agents you are responsible for.

Not a guarantee. A clean pink-crow result means "no violation was detected," not "this is safe." Deploy it as one layer of defense-in-depth alongside least-privilege tool design, sandboxing, and human review for high-impact actions — never as the only control.

Everything here is Apache-2.0. Inspect it, fork it, and hold it to the bar it asks of others. If you ship something built on pink-crow, we'd love to hear about it in the discussions.

Star it, then try to break it.
